Use a logo

Six logo directions for SimpleForms. Each starts from the idea of a black circle — a period, a filled checkbox, a focused input — and pushes it in a different direction.

01 — Void Circle

The most minimal mark possible. A solid black circle acts as a period, a filled radio button, and a stamp of completion.

02 — Form Field

A rounded input field with the cursor collapsed into a solid circle. Instantly reads as "form" without needing a letter.

03 — Checked

A filled circle with a white checkmark. A nod to completed forms, confirmation, and frictionless submission.

04 — Data Points

Four dots converge into a central circle. Represents form responses flowing into one place — your spreadsheet.

05 — Simple S

A white "S" path carved through the black circle. Connects the shape to the SimpleForms name while staying iconic.

06 — Target

Concentric circles suggest focus and precision. The outer mass keeps it bold at small sizes.
